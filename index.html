LECTURE 8 · WIRELESS NETWORK SECURITY

The cable is gone.
The attack surface is not.

Wireless networking makes access convenient because communication travels through radio frequency. That same convenience creates exposure: signals can be discovered, captured, interfered with, impersonated, or attacked from outside the physical building.
